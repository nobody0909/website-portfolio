About Me

I help businesses transform scattered financial and operational data into clear, actionable insights that support better decision-making. By combining financial planning expertise with business intelligence, I design executive dashboards that reveal the metrics that matter most profitability, cash flow, sales performance, expenses, inventory, and operational efficiency. Rather than creating reports that simply display numbers, I build decision-support systems that help business owners, CEOs, CFOs, and finance teams quickly identify opportunities, uncover hidden risks, and monitor performance with confidence. Every dashboard is designed to answer critical business questions, reduce manual reporting, and provide a single source of truth for the organization. My approach combines financial analysis, data modeling, automation, and business strategy to turn complex data into meaningful insights that drive smarter decisions and sustainable growth.
